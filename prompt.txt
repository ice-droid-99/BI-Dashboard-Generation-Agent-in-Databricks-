---PAGE 1: OVERVIEW---
FILTERS (top of page, 3 slicers):
1. Date filter with two modes the user can toggle — one that filters by exact date, and one that filters by month and year combined (e.g. "Jan 2025"). Both should affect all visuals on the page.
2. Team filter — list of all sales teams.
3. Rep filter — list of all sales reps, ideally cascading so it only shows reps in the selected team.
KPI CARDS (single row below filters, 9 cards):
- Total gross enrollments
- Total net enrollments
- Average daily enrollments (total net enrollments divided by number of active days in the selected period)
- Early cancellations count
- Cancelled debt rate (cancelled debt as a % of gross enrolled debt)
- Best performing team (team name with highest net enrollments — show as a text/highlight card)
- Average days to cancel
- Total gross enrolled debt in dollars
- Average debt per enrollment (gross debt divided by gross enrollments)
VISUALS (below KPI row, 2 side by side):
Left — Bar chart: Team performance. For each team show two grouped bars — total gross enrollments and total gross enrolled debt. Use a dual axis if needed so both metrics are readable (debt in $M, enrollments as count). Label each bar with its value.
Right — Donut chart: Cancellation breakdown by reason. Show count and % for each reason. Use red/amber tones.
All visuals respond to the 3 slicers above.
Color rules: green ≥100% of target, amber 80–99%, red <80%. Cancellation metrics in red/amber. Enrollment bars in neutral blue. Debt bars in teal/slate.

---PAGE 2: MONTHLY GOALS & REP PERFORMANCE---

No filters on this page. Data shown for the full period.

VISUAL 1 — Monthly goal vs actual table:
A matrix/table with one row per month. Columns: Month, Enrollment Target, Actual Net Enrollments, % Achievement, Debt Target, Actual Net Debt, Debt % Achievement.
Color-code the two % achievement columns: green ≥100%, amber 80–99%, red <80%.
Add a totals row at the bottom.

VISUAL 2 — Rep performance scorecard table:
One row per sales rep. Columns: Rep Name, Team, Gross Enrollments, Net Enrollments, Gross Debt, Avg Debt per Enrollment, Early Cancellations, Cancel Rate %, Avg Days to Cancel.
Sort by Net Enrollments descending. Color the Cancel Rate % column: green <5%, amber 5–10%, red >10%. Bold the top rep row.
This table gives managers a single view to compare volume, quality, and churn risk across every rep.
